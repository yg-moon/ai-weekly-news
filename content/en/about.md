# About

There are too many sensational stories to follow the news every day, while the events that really matter repeat in much the same way all week. So once a week, this site picks only the stories you should not miss and puts them in one place.

## Issues

Every Monday at 8 AM KST, a new issue covers the past week, Monday to Sunday: **five stories each from Korea, the world and AI**. Each story says what happened and **why it matters**. The aim is that you understand what an event means, not only the facts, in one read.

Weekly issues are combined into **quarterly reviews**, and quarterly reviews into an **annual review**. These show trends that a single week cannot.

## Selection

It starts with **reading every headline of the week**. For Korea, that means 15 major news outlets. For the world, Wikipedia’s current events page and The Guardian. For AI, company announcements and tech media.

Stories are ranked by **how much coverage they got that week**: on how many days, and in how many outlets, the same story ran. When two are close, the one that is harder to reverse or affects more people comes first.

**Only what could be known that week** goes in. Outcomes that came out later are left out, so each issue stays a record of its week.

## Production

AI collects and summarizes the news. The selection criteria and the process are designed by a person. No person checks each item. Instead, figures, dates and quotes are checked against the original articles, and **every item links to its sources**, so you can check anything that looks doubtful.

The cost and time to make each issue are public on the [Stats](../stats/) page. All rules and code are on [GitHub](https://github.com/yg-moon/ai-weekly-news). If you find a mistake or have a suggestion, please use [Feedback](#feedback) below.
